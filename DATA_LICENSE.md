# Data licensing

This repository deliberately does **not** apply one blanket licence to every
file in `data/`. Scientific facts, curated records, source catalogues, cached
responses, and derived exports have different rights holders and conditions.

## Project-created material

Unless a file identifies another rights holder, Local Bubble's original
documentation, hand-authored model configuration, and original visual assets
are available under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Reuse requires attribution to **IncusLuminis / Local Bubble**, a link to the
licence, and an indication of changes.

## Third-party material

No permission is granted here to relicense, remove attribution from, or
redistribute third-party material beyond its upstream terms. Each included
source and its required credit is recorded in
[`THIRD_PARTY_DATA.md`](THIRD_PARTY_DATA.md). When a record combines several
sources, comply with every applicable source term.

`data/normalized/`, `web/public/data/scene.json`, and other derived exports
are factual/curated outputs. They may incorporate third-party database rights
or attribution duties; treat the provenance attached to each record and the
third-party notice as controlling.
